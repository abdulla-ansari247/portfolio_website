X-Ray Imaging Optimization

Research

My bachelor thesis: optimizing a multi-source X-ray imaging setup in my own C++ simulation on GEANT4, running 1,000,000 gamma-ray primaries per configuration. Models angled radiation sources and particle interactions to detect obscured structures within dense materials such as bone, with statistical analysis of the imaging outputs in Python to optimize source geometry and beam orientation against traditional single-source X-ray methods.
